# The Economic Impact of Algorithmic Dependency on India's Domestic Market

*How Foreign Ad Networks Control the Bridge Between Indian Businesses and Consumers.*

**Author:** Viraj Ravani

## Introduction

This research project examines how India's structural dependence on foreign digital platforms, advertising networks, and app store ecosystems influences domestic businesses, revenue distribution, and the broader digital economy.

As consumer discovery and digital commerce increasingly flow through global gatekeeper platforms, significant capital is captured through advertising expenditures and commission structures. This initiative highlights the vital importance of open domestic digital infrastructure, local economic value retention, and long-term digital sovereignty for India's growing market.

## Key Areas of Research

- **Foreign Digital Platform Dependency:** The concentration of digital discovery, search, and social media traffic within foreign platforms, and its structural impact on India's advertising ecosystem.
- **Economic Implications for Indian SMEs:** How rising customer acquisition costs (CAC) and digital ad spend compress profit margins for domestic small and medium enterprises.
- **App Store Commissions and Revenue Distribution:** The effect of closed mobile operating system billing rules and commission rates on local software developers and digital startups.
- **Digital Sovereignty and Open Protocols:** The strategic role of Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) and open network initiatives like ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) in unbundling discovery and retaining capital within the domestic economy.

## Interactive Simulator

The companion **India Digital Economy Simulator** allows researchers, policymakers, and visitors to experiment with economic assumptions:

- Adjust parameters including total digital ad spend, foreign platform capture rates, app store commissions, and domestic retention targets.
- Compare hypothetical policy and market scenarios, such as status-quo market concentration versus progressive adoption of open domestic protocols.
- Explore interactive flow visualisations, scenario comparisons, and sensitivity curves to observe how parameter adjustments influence projected capital retention.

## Purpose

The objective of this project is to make the economic implications of algorithmic dependency transparent and accessible through research, interactive analysis, and scenario modelling.

## Disclaimer

This project is an independent research and analytical initiative. Figures and outputs presented in the simulator may include illustrative assumptions and should not be interpreted as audited economic statistics, official government estimates or financial advice. The simulator demonstrates hypothetical scenarios and does not predict actual economic outcomes.
