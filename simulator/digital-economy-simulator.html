06 • RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

Methodology, Formulations & Caveats

ACADEMIC METHODOLOGY

1. Mathematical Formulations

Foreign Ad Capture: Ad Spend × (Foreign Capture % / 100)
Domestic Retention: Ad Spend × (Domestic Retention % / 100)
Unallocated Intermediary: Ad Spend − Foreign Revenue − Domestic Retention
App Store Outflow: App Store GMV × (Commission Rate % / 100)
Combined Gross Outflow: Foreign Ad Revenue + App Store Outflow

2. Core Research Caveats

  • Gross Commercial Flow ≠ Net Economic Drain: Modelled outflow reflects commercial transaction volume. Foreign tech platforms maintain Indian operating entities, contribute corporate and equalization levies, employ local engineering personnel, and provide commercial discovery utilities to domestic enterprises.
  • Separation of Economic Streams: Digital advertising expenditure and app marketplace transaction volumes are modeled as distinct flows to avoid double-counting.
  • Assumption-Driven Simulation: Results are parametric estimations designed to explore market structure scenarios, not audited balance-of-payments statistics.
